Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

ContentForge Uploader ("the app") is a personal automation tool operated by its sole owner. This policy explains how the app handles data, specifically regarding its use of the YouTube API Services.

Who uses this app

This app is used exclusively by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel(s), as well as Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. It is not distributed publicly and no third party accounts are authenticated through it.

What data the app accesses

Through the YouTube Data API, the app accesses only the data required to upload pre-produced videos to the owner's own YouTube channel(s), including video files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and privacy status. The app does not access, collect, or store data belonging to any other YouTube user, viewer, or channel.

How data is stored

OAuth credentials (access and refresh tokens) used to connect to YouTube are stored locally on the owner's private server and are never shared with third parties. No viewer or subscriber personal data is collected or retained.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can review Google's own privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy.

Data sharing

No data accessed by this app, including data from YouTube, is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party for any purpose.
